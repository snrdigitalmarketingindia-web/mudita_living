Developer Reference Snippet

This file exists so developers can copy the nav and footer into any new page with consistent markup and styles. The content between nav and footer is your page's unique content.

How to use this snippet

  • Copy everything between <!-- NAV: COPY FROM HERE --> and <!-- NAV: COPY TO HERE --> into the top of your new page's <body>.
  • Copy everything between <!-- FOOTER: COPY FROM HERE --> and <!-- FOOTER: COPY TO HERE --> just before your closing </body> tag.
  • Copy the nav and footer <style> rules into your page's <style> block, along with the brand token variables (:root { --burgundy: ... }).
  • Adjust all ../pages/ and ../assets/ paths to match your page's directory depth.
  • Include the Google Fonts <link> tag in your page <head>.
  • Add padding-top: 72px (or 112px) to your first content section to clear the fixed nav.
