Coaching

Executive Human Performance Coaching is a specialised form of professional coaching that works with the whole person — not just strategy or habits — to build sustainable, high-level performance. Rather than optimising how much you do, it focuses on the quality of how you operate: your energy, decisions, relationships, identity, and inner alignment. Radhika draws on the Pancha Kosha framework, neuroscience, and ICF-accredited coaching methodology to do this work.
Most life coaching works at the level of goals, mindset, and accountability. Radhika's approach goes deeper — integrating yoga philosophy, somatic awareness, and the Pancha Kosha model alongside formal ICF coaching methodology. This means the work addresses not just what you think, but how you carry yourself, what your body is telling you, and what values you're actually operating from (vs. the ones you'd like to be). It's also specifically designed for high-performing professionals — the language, pace, and depth reflect that context.
Menopause Coaching is specialist support for women navigating perimenopause and menopause — particularly high-achieving women who are managing significant professional responsibilities at the same time. It combines hormonal health education, emotional intelligence work, somatic practices, and practical workplace navigation. It is not therapy, and it works alongside (never against) any medical treatment you're receiving. It's for any woman in this transition who wants intelligent, compassionate support — and who refuses to disappear quietly into it.
There are no fixed-length packages — programmes are designed around each client's goals and context. That said, most 1:1 coaching engagements run for 3–6 months, with fortnightly sessions of 60–90 minutes. Some clients continue beyond that; others complete intensive shorter programmes. Radhika will give you her honest recommendation during your discovery call.
Yes — both. 1:1 coaching is available for individuals. Small group coaching programmes (typically 4–6 people) run periodically — these are curated carefully so participants are at a similar life stage or facing comparable challenges, which makes the group dynamic as valuable as the individual work. Dates and availability for group programmes are announced by email and on the website when they open.

Yoga

Traditional Hatha Yoga — drawn from the classical texts (Hatha Yoga Pradipika, Shiva Samhita, Gheranda Samhita) and taught within a lineage-based framework. This is not hot yoga, power yoga, vinyasa flow, or any modern fitness variant. Traditional Hatha is precise, methodical, and deeply intelligent — and it works at a level that most modern yoga doesn't reach. For therapeutic applications, Yoga Therapy sessions use asana, pranayama, mudra, meditation, and Yoga Nidra tailored to your specific condition.
Absolutely. Traditional Hatha is accessible to beginners — in fact, it's often easier to learn properly from the start than to unlearn habits acquired in less traditional settings. If you're new to yoga, Radhika's team will start with foundation practices and build from there. No prior experience, flexibility, or fitness level is required. The only prerequisite is genuine curiosity.
All online sessions are conducted live via Zoom — not pre-recorded. You'll need a mat, a reasonably quiet space, and a stable internet connection. Sessions are conducted in real-time so the teacher can observe, correct, and adjust what you're doing — the quality of guidance is comparable to in-person practice. You'll receive a Zoom link and any preparation notes by email before your first session.
In-person yoga sessions in Singapore are conducted by Radhika's trained specialist team at a central Singapore location (CBD area). The exact address is shared on booking confirmation. Sessions are available on weekdays and weekends. To enquire about availability, use the contact form or email hello@muditaliving.com.
Yoga Therapy is the intentional, individualised application of yoga practices for health and healing — distinct from a yoga class. It begins with a thorough intake consultation covering your health history, current conditions, lifestyle, and goals. From there, a bespoke therapeutic protocol is designed using asana, pranayama, mudra, meditation, and Yoga Nidra. It works with a wide range of conditions including chronic stress, anxiety, sleep disruption, hormonal imbalance, back pain, and nervous system dysregulation. It always works alongside (never against) any existing medical treatment.
