Menopause Coaching

For high-achieving women navigating perimenopause and menopause without losing their edge, their identity, or their sense of self. Evidence-based, deeply human support — with someone who won't treat this as a footnote.

Menopause doesn't announce itself neatly. It arrives in disrupted sleep, unexpected brain fog, a shorter fuse, and a feeling that you're somehow less than you were — even when your track record says otherwise.

Radhika's menopause coaching integrates hormonal health education, emotional intelligence work, somatic practices, and the practical reality of being a high-functioning woman in the middle of a significant biological transition. It is the support you deserved years ago.

  • Specialist training in menopause coaching
  • Hormonal intelligence — understand what's actually happening
  • Performance continuity through the transition
  • Identity and body image as everything shifts
  • Workplace navigation — what to say, what not to say
  • Nervous system regulation and sleep support
  • Yoga and breathwork practices tailored to hormonal phases

The Process

How We Work Together

Three stages. Fully personalised. No rush, no script.

1

Discovery Call

A free 30-minute conversation — no pitch, no pressure. Radhika listens, asks the questions that matter, and gives you an honest read on whether and how working together would help. If it's not the right fit, she'll say so — and often point you in a better direction.

2

Personalised Programme Design

No off-the-shelf packages. After the discovery call, Radhika designs a programme around your specific context — the pressures, the goals, the timeline, the budget. You'll know what you're working toward, why, and roughly how. Then you begin.

3

Ongoing Support & Accountability

Sessions are typically fortnightly via Zoom, with email support between. Radhika holds both the warmth and the rigour — she will celebrate your wins and hold you to what you said you wanted when you drift. The work deepens as trust builds.
