What Makes This Different

This is not fitness yoga.

Modern studio yoga and Traditional Hatha Yoga share a name and very little else. Here's the distinction — and why it matters.

Traditional Hatha Yoga

What this practice is

  • Rooted in classical texts — the Hatha Yoga Pradipika, Shiva Samhita, and Gheranda Samhita
  • Taught with rigour and reverence for the original lineage — not a fitness adaptation
  • Understood through the Pancha Kosha (five-layer body) model — physical, energetic, mental, wisdom, and spirit
  • Designed to actually do something — restore, regulate, and heal — not just perform something
  • Therapeutic at its core — suitable for stress, nervous system regulation, chronic conditions, and hormonal health
  • Personalised — each session is informed by who you are and what you need
  • Consistent teacher — same practitioner each session for genuine continuity of care
What This Is Not

What you won't find here

  • Hot yoga, power yoga, or any workout-framed class
  • Large group drop-in fitness sessions focused on calories or flexibility goals
  • Instagram-aesthetic postures prioritised over actual wellbeing
  • Pre-recorded content without live guidance and correction
  • A one-size-fits-all sequence delivered to whoever books
  • Practice disconnected from breath, energy, and inner states
  • Yoga stripped of its original philosophy and purpose

Hatha Yoga Pradipika

15th-century foundational text — the systematic guide to Hatha practice, pranayama, and energetic alignment.

The Pancha Kosha Model

Five-body framework from the Taittiriya Upanishad — physical, energetic, mental, wisdom, and bliss sheaths.

Yoga Therapy

Individualised therapeutic application of yoga practices — bespoke protocols for specific health conditions and goals.

Rooted in Classical Lineage

Every session is understood through the five-layer body model — physical, energetic, mental, wisdom, and spirit. This means your practice reaches further than the poses, into how you actually function as a whole person.

Yoga as Medicine, Applied with Precision

Yoga Therapy is the intentional, individualised application of yoga practices for health, wellbeing, and healing. It is not a class with modifications — it is a bespoke therapeutic protocol, designed around your specific condition and goals. Sessions draw on asana, pranayama, mudra, meditation, and Yoga Nidra as appropriate.

🤝

Continuity & Genuine Guidance

You practice with the same teacher each session. Real-time correction, attentive guidance, and the kind of relationship that allows your practice to deepen over time. Small groups are capped at 6. Private sessions are entirely one-on-one.
