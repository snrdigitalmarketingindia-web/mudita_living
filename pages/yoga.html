What Makes This Different

This is not the yoga you've tried before.

Modern studio yoga and Traditional Hatha Yoga share a name and very little else. Here's the distinction — and why it matters.

📜

Rooted in Classical Lineage

Traditional Hatha Yoga is drawn from the original texts — the Hatha Yoga Pradipika, the Shiva Samhita, and the Gheranda Samhita. Not a fitness adaptation. The real thing, taught with the rigour and reverence it deserves.

🧬

The Pancha Kosha Lens

Every session is understood through the five-layer body model — physical, energetic, mental, wisdom, and spirit. This means your practice reaches further than the poses, into how you actually function as a whole person.

🩺

Therapeutic at the Core

Whether you're managing stress, regulating a nervous system running in overdrive, working with a chronic condition, or supporting hormonal health — this yoga is designed to do something, not just perform something.

Yoga Therapy

Yoga as medicine.
Applied with precision.

Yoga Therapy is the intentional, individualised application of yoga practices for health, wellbeing, and healing. It is not a yoga class with modifications — it is a bespoke therapeutic protocol, designed around your specific condition and goals.

Radhika draws on her training in Yoga Therapy and her understanding of the Pancha Kosha model to design sessions that address the root — not just the symptom. Whether you're working with stress, anxiety, chronic pain, hormonal disruption, or burnout, there is a yoga therapy approach for it.

Yoga Therapy Session

How a Yoga Therapy session works

  • Initial consultation — deep assessment of your health history, goals, and current state
  • Bespoke practice protocol — asana, pranayama, mudra, meditation, and Yoga Nidra as appropriate
  • Home practice — simple daily practices to support between sessions
  • Ongoing refinement — protocols evolve as your condition and capacity change
  • Available 1:1 online globally and in-person Singapore
