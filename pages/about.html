Her Story

Built from
the inside out

radhika-story.jpg
🇸🇬 Based in Singapore · Global practice

Radhika Avanthsa grew up understanding early that success and fulfilment are not the same thing. Born and raised in Singapore, she moved through a high-achieving life — the kind that looks right from the outside — before something quieter began asking a different question. Not "how do I do more?" but "who am I without the doing?"

That question led her into a serious study of Traditional Hatha Yoga — not the studio variety, but the classical lineage practice rooted in the original texts. She trained rigorously, and what she found there wasn't mysticism. It was a precise, evidence-aligned science of the self: a way of understanding how the body, breath, mind, and identity are connected — and how misalignment in one layer quietly undermines everything above it.

"High functioning on the outside, running on empty within. That's not a character flaw — it's a signal. And it's exactly where the real work begins."

She went on to complete her ICF accreditation, specialising in Executive Human Performance — because she kept meeting the same person in different offices. The executive who was technically succeeding but felt increasingly hollow. The entrepreneur who had built what they wanted and still couldn't rest. The senior leader navigating menopause while holding a team together, with no space to acknowledge what was actually happening. These aren't edge cases. These are the majority of high-performing people she meets.

Today, Radhika works globally — online and in person in Singapore — bringing the rigour of ancient wisdom and the clarity of modern coaching methodology to clients who are ready to stop managing their lives from the surface and start building from the inside out. Not fixing. Realigning.

Coaching Philosophy

Not fixing — realigning.

Radhika's work is built on a simple but radical premise: the people who come to her are not broken. They are misaligned. And there's a profound difference between the two.

The Framework

The Pancha Kosha Model — Five Layers of the Self

Ancient Vedic wisdom meets modern coaching — every Mudita Living programme is designed through this lens.

1
Annamaya Kosha
Physical Body

The body you live in. Breath, movement, somatic intelligence, physical regulation.

2
Pranamaya Kosha
Energy Body

Vital force and breath. The bridge between body and mind — and the fastest route to regulation.

3
Manomaya Kosha
Mental Body

Thoughts, emotions, patterns of perception. Where most coaching lives — and where most people are stuck.

4
Vijnanamaya Kosha
Wisdom Body

Discernment, values, identity. The capacity to know what is true — and act from it.

5
Anandamaya Kosha
Bliss Body

The deepest layer — pure awareness. Wholeness that doesn't depend on conditions.
